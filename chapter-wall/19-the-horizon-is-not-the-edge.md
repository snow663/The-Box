# Chapter 19 — The Horizon Is Not the Edge

## Core claim
A boundary of observation can easily masquerade as a boundary of existence.

## Material on the wall
- Observable-universe horizon as metaphor, used accurately.
- **Finding a horizon doesn’t mean you’ve found the edge of reality. It means you’ve found the edge of what your present position allows you to see.**
- Apply to instruments, historical records, sensory limits, conceptual vocabulary.
- Absence beyond a horizon must not automatically become nonexistence.

## Questions
- Research cosmology carefully so metaphor does not distort actual physics.
