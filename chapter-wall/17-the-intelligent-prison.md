# Chapter 17 — The Intelligent Prison

## Core claim
Boxes exist at multiple scales: individuals, professions, disciplines, institutions, cultures, civilizations.

## Material on the wall
- A civilization can become more capable while becoming more sophisticated at defending a foundational misconception.
- Shared vocabulary can create shared blind spots.
- Tools expand perception but tools are designed according to models.
- **Greater intelligence doesn’t necessarily eliminate conceptual blindness. It can make conceptual blindness extraordinarily sophisticated.**

## Questions
- Avoid turning “the Box” into an explanation for every social disagreement.
