# Chapter 5 — Nothing Enters Alone

## Core claim
Adding knowledge is not like dropping another object into a warehouse. Every addition creates or alters relationships.

## Material on the wall
- Knowledge is a network, not a pile.
- Add X and A/B/C/D may all acquire new meanings.
- A discovery can strengthen old conclusions, weaken them, or make previously unrelated observations suddenly relevant.
- **The Box doesn’t merely constrain which new ideas we’re willing to accept. Its contents continuously redefine one another.**
- Analogy: adding a load to a structure redistributes forces throughout the structure.

## Questions
- Use a mundane example before a scientific one.
- Distinguish adding raw observation from adding an interpretation.
