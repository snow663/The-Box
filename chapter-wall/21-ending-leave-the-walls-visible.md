# Ending — Leave the Walls Visible

## Core claim
The goal is not freedom from models. It is awareness of their provisional boundaries and disciplined willingness to revise them.

## Material on the wall
- Know which pieces are observations.
- Know which are interpretations.
- Know what depends on what.
- Know where the walls are.
- When something “impossible” lands outside: neither worship it nor dismiss it. Measure it.
- **Maybe intelligence is not ultimately measured by how much of reality we can place inside our sandbox. Maybe it is measured by whether we can recognize the moment reality becomes larger than it.**
- Greatest mistake may not be being wrong about what is inside the Box, but forgetting the Box was ours in the first place.

## Possible final callback
Return to a joke or mundane failure from early in the book, demonstrating that humility need not be solemn.
