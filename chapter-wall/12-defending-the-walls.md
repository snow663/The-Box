# Chapter 12 — Defending the Walls

## Core claim
Resistance to disruptive evidence can emerge naturally from a successful interconnected model; conspiracy or stupidity is not required.

## Material on the wall
- The more conclusions depend on premise P, the higher the cost of questioning P.
- Institutions also have Boxes: disciplines, methods, terminology, incentives, training.
- Rational conservatism is useful because most revolutionary claims are wrong.
- The same conservatism can become pathological if no possible evidence is permitted to challenge the premise.
- Ask: what observation would make us change our minds?

## Questions
- Handle sociology carefully; avoid cheap “experts are closed-minded” framing.
