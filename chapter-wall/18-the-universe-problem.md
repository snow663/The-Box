# Chapter 18 — The Universe Problem

## Core claim
We cannot completely step outside the system used to understand the system.

## Material on the wall
- We cannot leave the universe to inspect what contains it.
- We cannot leave cognition behind to inspect cognition without using cognition.
- Instruments, equations, language, philosophy—all originate within the system being investigated.
- **Every attempt to escape the sandbox constructs another sandbox.**
- Link to loose piece: `notes/thinking-outside-the-box.md`.
- “Thinking outside the box” creates a larger Box; now decide what to unpack from the old one.

## Questions
- Distinguish epistemic limitation from claims that external reality is unknowable.
