# Chapter 11 — Impossible Relative to What?

## Core claim
“Incompatible with our model” and “incompatible with reality” are different propositions.

## Material on the wall
- **Never confuse incompatibility with our current model for incompatibility with reality itself.**
- “Impossible” requires specifying the premises under which impossibility is derived.
- A violation of a model may indicate bad evidence, a misunderstood boundary condition, or an incomplete model.
- This does *not* imply logical contradictions can become true.

## Questions
- Separate physical impossibility, logical impossibility, statistical improbability, and practical impossibility.
