# Chapter 10 — The Anomaly

## Core claim
Most anomalies are errors. The philosophically important anomaly is the one that survives increasingly hostile attempts to kill it.

## Material on the wall
- Instruments drift. Samples contaminate. Witnesses misremember. People lie. Statistics mislead. Coincidences happen.
- Skepticism is essential.
- “Destroy the claim if it deserves to be destroyed.”
- Interesting moment: calibration checks out, replication succeeds, independent methods converge.
- **Then the anomaly is no longer the only object under examination. The Box is.**
- Possible opening joke: before overthrowing physics, check whether someone plugged the sensor in backward.

## Questions
- Define a sensible ladder of anomaly verification.
