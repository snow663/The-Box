# Chapter 2 — Everything Is Relative to What’s in the Box

## Core claim
Facts do not sit in isolation. Their apparent meaning, plausibility, normality, and importance emerge through relationships with other contents of the model.

## Material on the wall
- “Impossible” is often shorthand for “incompatible with the other things currently in here.”
- Reference frame is inside the system being measured.
- New item X can change the interpreted relationship among A, B, C, and D.
- A fact may remain observationally unchanged while its *meaning* changes.
- Possible central line: **The human mind does not perceive the boundary of reality. It perceives the boundary of reality relative to what it has already placed inside the Box.**
- Avoid philosophical relativism: reality is not being claimed to depend on belief; our *judgment* of it does.

## Questions
- Develop a simple physical/mechanical analogy where the same component means something different after another component is discovered.
