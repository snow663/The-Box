# Chapter 13 — Rearranging the Box

## Core claim
A sufficiently fundamental correction does not merely add or remove a fact. It rearranges relationships among existing facts.

## Material on the wall
- New fact → relationships change → old evidence reinterpreted → model reorganizes.
- Structural/load-bearing analogy.
- **A sufficiently fundamental mistake wouldn’t just add or remove a fact. It could rearrange the Box.**
- Paradigm shift should be explained mechanically rather than mystically.

## Questions
- Find visualizable examples where old facts remain but their arrangement changes.
