# Chapter 3 — The Invisible Wall

## Core claim
A model becomes dangerous when its boundaries stop looking like properties of the model and start looking like properties of reality.

## Material on the wall
- “I don’t know how this could happen.”
- “According to our present understanding, this shouldn’t happen.”
- “This did not happen.”
- These statements are not equivalent.
- The Box becomes invisible precisely because it works so well.
- Successful prediction encourages justified confidence; repeated success can quietly convert confidence into ontology.
- Possible line: **The old Box wasn’t useless. We had mistaken a locally excellent description for the complete description.**
- Possible line: **The universe has no obligation to remain inside the boundary of what any particular generation considers reasonable.**

## Questions
- Find an example where “unknown mechanism” was historically treated as “impossible event.”
