# Chapter 15 — The Bargain of Skepticism

## Core claim
Skepticism has reciprocal obligations. Demanding extraordinary evidence means accepting the implications if extraordinary evidence actually arrives.

## Material on the wall
- **If extraordinary evidence actually arrives, an extraordinary conclusion must be allowed onto the table.**
- Otherwise skepticism has become a belief-preservation mechanism.
- Evidence threshold should scale with claim magnitude, but threshold cannot secretly be infinite.
- **If reality proves me wrong, I will follow it there.**

## Questions
- Formalize difference between healthy skepticism and unfalsifiable rejection.
