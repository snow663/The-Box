# Chapter 8 — The Coherent Lie

## Core claim
Internal consistency is valuable evidence, but it is not identical to correspondence with reality.

## Material on the wall
- Multiple conclusions can agree because they share the same hidden premise.
- Intelligence can make error harder to recognize because intelligence can make error more coherent.
- A model may explain every observation available to it and still be incomplete.
- Avoid implying coherence is weak evidence; it is often excellent evidence. The point is that shared dependency matters.
