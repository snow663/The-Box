# The Box

**A philosophy of knowledge, certainty, and the invisible boundaries of thought**

*The Box* examines a simple problem with enormous consequences: human beings do not encounter reality without a reference frame. We interpret new information relative to what we already know, and the model that gives us intellectual power can quietly become the boundary of what we believe reality permits.

This repository is the working manuscript.

## Central premise

> The human mind does not perceive the boundary of reality. It perceives the boundary of reality relative to what it has already placed inside the box.

The Box is not something to escape or destroy. Cognition requires models. The problem begins when we forget the walls are ours.
